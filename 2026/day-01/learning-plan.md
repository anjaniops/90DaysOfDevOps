## 🧠 My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud

DevOps is the practice of automating and improving the software delivery lifecycle by integrating development and operations.

It focuses on:
- Continuous Integration & Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)
- Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
- Monitoring & reliability
- Collaboration between teams

Cloud Engineering enables scalable, on-demand infrastructure using platforms like AWS, making systems highly available and fault-tolerant.
